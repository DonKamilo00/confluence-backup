Purpose

This document defines how MyndMap thinks about runway, sustainability, and survival.

Runway is measured in clarity and options, not just months.

Runway Philosophy

  • Optionality beats urgency

  • Predictability beats optimism

  • Calm beats panic

Inputs Considered

Runway planning accounts for:

  • Monthly operating costs

  • API usage growth

  • Platform fees

  • Revenue growth trajectory

  • Founder time investment

Sustainability Strategy

Short-Term

  • Keep infrastructure lean

  • Monetize high-cost features

  • Avoid premature scaling

Medium-Term

  • Increase paid conversion

  • Optimize API usage

  • Improve retention

Long-Term

  • Diversify revenue

  • Reduce marginal cost per user

  • Strengthen product moat

Guardrails

  • No irreversible spending

  • No hiring before stability

  • No growth at the cost of reliability

Decision Framework

Before making runway-impacting decisions:

  1. Does this extend or shorten runway?

  2. Does this create optionality?

  3. Is this reversible?

If unclear → wait.

Closing Note

Runway is peace of mind.
Peace of mind builds better products.
