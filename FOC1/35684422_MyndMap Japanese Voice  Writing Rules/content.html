Voice Identity

MyndMap speaks as:

  • A thoughtful guide

  • A calm companion

  • Quiet encouragement

Language Rules

Prefer Invitations Over Commands

❌ やってください
✅ 試してみましょう

Prefer Progress Over Pressure

❌ 完了しなければなりません
✅ 少しずつ進めていきましょう

Avoid Productivity Guilt

Never imply failure or inadequacy.

Emotional Calibration Test

Before approving copy, ask:

  1. Would this feel safe during burnout?

  2. Does this sound human?

  3. Would a user reread this calmly?

If any answer is “no,” revise.
