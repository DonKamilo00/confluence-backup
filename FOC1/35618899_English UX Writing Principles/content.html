Objective

English copy must reduce thinking effort.

Every sentence should lower cognitive load rather than increase it.

Rule 1 — One Idea Per Sentence

Avoid stacking concepts.

❌ Organize your goals and improve productivity by creating structured plans.
✅ Break goals into clear steps.

Rule 2 — Prefer Concrete Language

Abstract wording increases friction.

❌ Optimize workflow efficiency
✅ Focus on what matters next

Rule 3 — Shorter is Calmer

Length creates perceived effort.

Guideline:

  • UI text: ideally under 12 words

  • Descriptions: under 2 lines when possible

Rule 4 — Remove Invisible Pressure

Avoid performance framing.

Avoid:

  • Stay productive

  • Maximize output

  • Don’t fall behind

Prefer:

  • Start small

  • Continue when ready

  • Progress builds over time

Rule 5 — Reduce Authority Tone

MyndMap guides rather than commands.

❌ Complete this task now
✅ Ready to finish this task?
