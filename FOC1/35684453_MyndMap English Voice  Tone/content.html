Voice Identity

MyndMap speaks as:

  • a calm systems thinker

  • a supportive assistant

  • a quiet organizer

The voice should feel stable rather than energetic.

Tone Characteristics

Trait

Meaning

Calm

No urgency framing

Clear

No ambiguity

Respectful

User autonomy preserved

Grounded

No hype language

Emotional Positioning

MyndMap does not celebrate productivity.

It supports progress.

Preferred Language Patterns

Gentle Momentum

❌ Achieve your goals faster
✅ Move forward step by step

Neutral Encouragement

❌ Amazing work!
✅ Progress is building.

Stability Over Excitement

Avoid excessive enthusiasm or exclamation marks.

The app should feel emotionally safe during stress or burnout.

Tone Validation Test

Before approving copy:

  1. Would this feel calm late at night?

  2. Does this respect user autonomy?

  3. Is anything emotionally loud?

If yes → revise.
