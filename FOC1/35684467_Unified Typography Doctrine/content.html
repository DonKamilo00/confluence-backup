Purpose

Typography in MyndMap is intentionally standardized across platforms using a controlled font system rather than relying entirely on operating system defaults.

MyndMap uses:

  • Hiragino Sans for Japanese text

  • Roboto for English and Latin text

This creates consistent emotional tone and reading rhythm across iOS, Android, and tablet environments.

Core Principle

Typography is part of emotional UX, not decoration.

Font choice influences:

  • perceived calmness

  • cognitive effort

  • reading speed

  • trust in the interface

The typographic system must therefore remain stable across languages and devices.

Font System

Japanese — Hiragino Sans (ヒラギノ角ゴ)

Hiragino Sans is selected as the primary Japanese typeface across MyndMap.

Characteristics

  • Balanced stroke weight

  • High legibility at small UI sizes

  • Soft visual rhythm

  • Familiar appearance to Japanese users

Emotional Effect

  • Calm

  • Supportive

  • Native-feeling

  • Non-clinical

Usage

  • All Japanese UI text

  • Labels

  • AI responses

  • Educational content

English / Latin — Roboto

Roboto is used as the primary Latin typeface.

Characteristics

  • Neutral geometric structure

  • High readability on mobile displays

  • Excellent performance across platforms

  • Predictable rendering behavior

Emotional Effect

  • Clear

  • Stable

  • Functional without feeling corporate

Why Not System Defaults

MyndMap intentionally avoids relying solely on platform typography because:

  1. Emotional consistency is prioritized over platform variation.

  2. Multilingual layouts require predictable spacing.

  3. AI-generated text must render consistently.

  4. Cross-platform screenshots and marketing visuals remain aligned.

Cross-Language Harmony

Roboto and Hiragino Sans were chosen because they coexist well visually:

  • similar perceived weight

  • compatible spacing rhythm

  • balanced visual density

Principle:

Switching language should not change the emotional atmosphere of the interface.

Platform Behaviour Notes

iOS

  • Smoother rendering

  • Slightly softer appearance

Android

  • Sharper edge rendering

  • Slightly denser visual perception

Design adjustments should rely on spacing and layout — not font substitution.

Implementation Rules

  • Do not introduce additional fonts without doctrine approval.

  • Avoid mixing multiple Latin fonts.

  • Avoid decorative or expressive typefaces.

  • Typography must remain invisible to the user.

Goal:
Users notice clarity, not fonts.
