Typography as Emotional Design

Typography influences:

  • Cognitive load

  • Trust perception

  • Emotional warmth

  • Reading fatigue

For Japanese interfaces, font choice strongly affects perceived intent.

iOS Typography

Font: Hiragino Sans (ヒラギノ角ゴ)

System default Japanese font on Apple platforms.

Characteristics

  • Balanced stroke weight

  • High legibility

  • Familiar to Japanese users

  • Neutral emotional tone

Effect

  • Calm

  • Native

  • Trustworthy

Principle:

Hiragino Sans represents the intended MyndMap reading experience baseline.

Android Typography

Font: Roboto + System Japanese Fallback

Roboto primarily supports Latin scripts.

Japanese characters depend on OEM fallback fonts.

Observed Effects

  • Slightly sharper appearance

  • Increased visual density

  • Less organic flow than iOS rendering

Mitigation Guidelines

  • Increase line height

  • Avoid dense paragraph blocks

  • Prefer short supportive sentences

  • Use whitespace intentionally
