Purpose

The Fountain of Calligraphy defines how language, tone, and typography function as part of the MyndMap user experience.

Within MyndMap, text is not treated as content alone — it is part of the emotional interface layer.

This fountain ensures:

  • Japanese language feels native rather than translated

  • Emotional tone remains calm and supportive

  • Typography reinforces cognitive clarity

  • Platform rendering differences do not alter intent

Core Principle

Poor nuance kills retention silently.

Users rarely report linguistic discomfort.
Instead, they disengage gradually through reduced session time and lower trust.

Language quality therefore directly influences:

  • Retention

  • Emotional safety

  • AI credibility

  • App Store reviews

  • Perceived product maturity

Scope

This fountain governs:

  • Japanese copywriting standards

  • Tone consistency rules

  • Politeness calibration

  • Typography selection

  • Cross-platform rendering behavior

  • Linguistic QA processes

Design Philosophy

MyndMap language should feel like:

  • A quiet guide

  • A supportive peer

  • Calm reassurance

Not:

  • A productivity authority

  • A clinical directive

  • A parental instruction system
