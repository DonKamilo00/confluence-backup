Epic Alignment

Epic: Japanese Language Depth Pass
Objective: Audit Japanese copy for linguistic authenticity and emotional alignment.

Audit Categories

1. Natural Phrasing

Avoid literal English translation structures.

Japanese UX language prioritizes flow and implication rather than instruction.

Example

❌ タスクを完了してください
(Literal command)

✅ このタスクを終わらせてみましょう
(Gentle invitation)

Principle:

Suggestion reduces psychological resistance.

2. Politeness Level Consistency

Target Tone:

丁寧体（polite form）with softness

Avoid extremes:

Too Formal

Too Casual

Clinical distance

Loss of trust

Institutional feel

App feels immature

Desired perception:

supportive companion

3. Emotional Tone

All copy must remain:

  • Non-judgmental

  • Calm

  • Encouraging

  • Shame-free

Avoid wording implying failure or pressure.

Examples to avoid:

  • 急いでください (hurry)

  • 必ず (must)

  • 今すぐ (immediate urgency)

Why This Matters

Research in behavioral UX shows emotional friction increases abandonment even when functionality is correct.

For ADHD users specifically:

  • directive language increases avoidance response.

Intended Effect

  • Longer session duration

  • Reduced anxiety during task interaction

  • Higher Japanese review sentiment

  • Increased perceived empathy
