Purpose

Provide a fast validation system for approving English copy.

Reviewers should be able to evaluate text in under 10 seconds.

The 5-Point Check

1. Immediate Understanding

Can a user understand this without rereading?

2. Calm Tone

Does this avoid urgency or pressure?

3. Short Enough

Can any words be removed?

4. Non-Judgmental

Does this avoid implying failure?

5. Human Sounding

Would a calm person say this aloud?

Automatic Rejection Signals

  • motivational slogans

  • corporate phrasing

  • excessive enthusiasm

  • long explanatory paragraphs

Outcome

Consistent English voice across:

  • UI

  • AI responses

  • onboarding

  • future features
