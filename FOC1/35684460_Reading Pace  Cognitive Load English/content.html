Purpose

Reading pace determines whether users feel calm or overwhelmed.

English copy controls interaction speed inside MyndMap.

Core Principle

Faster reading does not equal better understanding.

ADHD users benefit from predictable pacing rather than dense information.

Pacing Guidelines

Prefer Sequential Information

❌ Multiple instructions in one block
✅ Step-by-step presentation

Use Visual Breathing Room

  • Break text into small groups

  • Avoid stacked explanations

  • Allow pauses between concepts

Sentence Rhythm

Ideal rhythm:

  • short statement

  • pause (whitespace)

  • next action

This creates psychological forward motion.

Cognitive Load Reduction

Avoid:

  • multi-clause sentences

  • technical terminology

  • explanatory overload

Prefer:

  • direct meaning

  • familiar words

  • progressive disclosure
