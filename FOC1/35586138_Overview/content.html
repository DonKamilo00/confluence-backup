Purpose

The English Calligraphy Doctrine defines how English language is written, structured, and experienced within MyndMap.

English serves as the structural language layer of the platform.
It establishes clarity, logic, and interaction understanding from which all other localizations derive.

Unlike Japanese, which emphasizes emotional nuance, English prioritizes:

  • immediate comprehension

  • cognitive simplicity

  • calm instruction

  • reduced mental effort

Core Principle

English creates clarity before emotion.

If English copy becomes complex or ambiguous, localization quality degrades across all languages.

Role of English in MyndMap

English defines:

  • feature explanations

  • onboarding clarity

  • AI reasoning structure

  • task understanding

  • system feedback messages

It should never introduce unnecessary interpretation.

Design Philosophy

English should feel:

  • calm

  • precise

  • grounded

  • human

  • minimal

Not:

  • motivational

  • corporate

  • productivity-driven

  • overly clinical

User Experience Goal

Users should think:

“I immediately understand what this means.”

without feeling instructed or pressured.
