This Cookie Policy explains how MyndMap Inc. LTD (“MyndMap”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and within certain web-based features of the MyndMap app.

We believe in transparency.
You deserve to know exactly what is collected, why it matters, and how you can control it.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.
They help websites function, remember preferences, and understand how visitors interact with the site.

Cookies are not harmful.
They cannot access your files or install anything.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to maintain clarity, functionality, and stability across our systems.

We use cookies for:

Essential Operations

  • Enabling core website functions

  • Maintaining security

  • Managing sessions and login states

Performance & Analytics

  • Understanding how users engage with our site

  • Improving navigation, content, and usability

  • Monitoring load times and errors

Preferences

  • Saving language selections

  • Remembering user settings

  • Maintaining accessibility preferences

We do not use cookies for:

  • Advertising

  • Selling data

  • Third-party behavioural tracking

  • Personal profiling not directly related to service improvement

3. Types of Cookies We Use

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are required for the website to function.
They cannot be disabled because the site will not operate without them.

Examples:

  • Session management

  • Login/authentication

  • Security controls

2. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how people use the website.

Examples:

  • Page views

  • Feature engagement

  • Error reports and crash diagnostics

Analytics is anonymous and used only to improve the experience.

3. Preference Cookies

These remember choices you’ve made.

Examples:

  • Language (EN/JP)

  • Layout settings

  • Cookie acceptance banner preferences

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted third-party tools such as:

  • Analytics providers (e.g., privacy-focused analytics)

  • Infrastructure services (e.g., CDN providers)

These providers may place cookies to help us understand performance and ensure service stability.

We do not allow advertisers, tracking networks, or marketing partners to place cookies on our site.

5. Cookies in the MyndMap App

The MyndMap mobile app uses minimal tracking and does not rely on browser cookies.
However, the web sections of the app (e.g., account pages, support pages, documentation) may use:

  • Authentication tokens

  • Session cookies

  • Analytics cookies for performance monitoring

These are strictly functional or performance-based.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You are in control.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block cookies

  • Delete existing cookies

  • Set notifications before a cookie is stored

  • Disable third-party cookies

  • Set privacy levels per website

Instructions:
Search “manage cookies” + your browser name (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

If you disable essential cookies, some parts of the website may not function correctly.

7. Legal Basis for Using Cookies

For visitors in the UK and EU:

  • Essential cookies are used under “legitimate interest.”

  • Analytics and preference cookies require consent via the cookie banner.

For global users, we follow the same standard of transparency.

8. How Long Cookies Are Stored

Cookies may be:

  • Session cookies – deleted when you close your browser

  • Persistent cookies – remain until they expire or you delete them

We aim to use short-lived cookies wherever possible.
