Purpose of This Document

This page defines who is responsible for what at MyndMap.

Clear responsibility:

  • Reduces cognitive overhead

  • Prevents duplicated effort

  • Enables calm execution during releases

  • Makes scaling the team straightforward

Roles are defined by ownership and accountability, not titles alone.

Operating Principle

Every operational surface must have a clearly defined owner.

If something breaks, it should be immediately obvious who is responsible for identifying, fixing, or escalating it.

Operating Model

MyndMap currently operates under a Two-Founder Model.

Both founders hold distinct, complementary responsibilities with minimal overlap to avoid ambiguity.
