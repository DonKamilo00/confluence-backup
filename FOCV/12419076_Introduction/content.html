Computer Vision (CV) is one of the most rapidly growing areas of research nowadays, its based around using the machine learning to give the machines ability to see, capture or process the visual information from our world, some of the most popular use cases involve Autonomous cars like tesla enabling the car to perceive the environment and objects like people, cars or road signs using the 8 cameras.

This is only a small example of how computer vision can be applied, as the field itself is very broad. For instance, some areas involve working solely with images, known as image processing. Adobe’s AI-powered features are a great example of this: their goal is to use trained models to augment or modify images in various ways. It’s important to note that image processing traditionally refers to the manipulation of images and does not necessarily require AI, although many modern computer vision workflows do use AI for preprocessing. One example in Adobe Photoshop is image expansion, which allows users to extend an existing image using generative AI.

The mission of this Fountain will be to explore potential applications for MyndMap and provide insight into how CV can be used in the context of productivity by offering a variety of resources, such as articles and research papers.
