Fountain of Development is MyndMap’s engineering playbook for building the app in a clean, efficient, and scalable way using React Native (Expo). It documents best practices, architectural patterns, tooling standards, and performance principles that guide day-to-day development. This space exists to keep the codebase maintainable, the developer experience calm, and the product reliable as MyndMap evolves across iOS and Android—so contributors can ship confidently without re-learning hard-won lessons.
