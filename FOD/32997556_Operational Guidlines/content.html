Purpose

This page defines operational discipline around Firebase changes.

When Not to Use Firebase

Do not use Firebase for:

  • Complex business logic

  • Expensive aggregations

  • AI processing

  • One-off experiments

Breaking Changes

  • Must be documented

  • Must be reviewed

  • Must be reversible where possible

No silent migrations.

Change Coordination

Firebase changes:

  • Are coordinated with releases

  • Are tested on both platforms

  • Are documented in Confluence

Golden Rule

Firebase changes are product changes.

Treat them accordingly.
