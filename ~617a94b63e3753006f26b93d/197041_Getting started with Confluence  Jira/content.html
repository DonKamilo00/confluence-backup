Learn more about Confluence with these quick tips

Find (or create) your team space

Confluence is made up of team spaces. Spaces are where teams organize their ideas, projects, and documentation. Spaces can be organized with folders, and you can drag-and-drop, or nest content to re-order it too.

Find the space your team is working in - if it doesn't exist, you can always create a new one.

Express your thinking through different content types

Pages

Pages start as drafts that can be published into source-of-truth knowledge for your team — ideal for sharing policies, guides, and playbooks.

Live Docs

Live Docs let you collaborate in realtime with your team for better ideas, faster decisions, and unified edits – all without needing to publish.

Whiteboards

Whiteboards are a place to brainstorm, build connections, and make ideas come to life.

Databases

Databases enable teams to build a library of information, track deliverables, and aggregate tools, links, and information from within and outside Confluence into a customizable view.

Smart Links

Smart links allow users to integrate and display content from other tools and platforms directly within Confluence, centralizing information in one accessible location.

Make your content more engaging with elements

Elements are the building blocks that help you organize and present information in the way you want. Elements include dates, status lozenges, panels, Confluence macros, Marketplace macros, and more. Type / to see a list of elements.

Collaborate with your teammates using comments and reactions

Comments initiate conversations, enabling you to add information, ask questions, and foster collaboration. Highlight text and selectComment in the pop-up menu for inline comments.

If someone creates good content on Confluence, leave a reaction to acknowledge it.
