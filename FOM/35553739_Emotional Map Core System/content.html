MM: Moments should follow a repeating emotional cycle.

Stage 1 — Self (Regulation)

Focus: internal pause.

Examples:

  • window portraits

  • quiet mornings

  • solitary reflection

Emotional message:

slowing down is allowed.

Stage 2 — Environment (Grounding)

Focus: nature or empty space.

Examples:

  • water ripples

  • grass movement

  • empty rooms

Message:

the world moves gently.

Stage 3 — Parallel Presence (Coexistence)

Focus: multiple people sharing space quietly.

Examples:

  • reading together

  • separate activities

  • shared silence

Message:

connection doesn’t require performance.

Stage 4 — Shared Stillness (Connection)

Focus: companionship moments.

Examples:

  • sunset silhouettes

  • walking together

  • quiet companionship

Message:

presence is enough.

Stage 5 — Return (Reset)

Return to solitude imagery.

This resets the emotional loop.
