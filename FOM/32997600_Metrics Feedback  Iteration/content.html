Purpose

This page defines how marketing effectiveness is measured.

Metrics inform — they do not dictate.

Metrics That Matter

  • Retention

  • Engagement depth

  • Conversion quality

  • User feedback

Vanity Metrics to Ignore

  • Raw impressions

  • Follower count

  • Likes without context

Feedback Loops

  • App reviews

  • Emails

  • Qualitative responses

  • Community sentiment

Iteration Rules

  • Reflect monthly

  • Adjust calmly

  • Avoid overreacting

Golden Rule

If a metric doesn’t change decisions,
stop tracking it.
