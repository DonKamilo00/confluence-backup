Purpose

This page documents MyndMap’s collaboration with Dr. Misuzu Nakashima and defines how clinical insight informs product design.

Authority Loops exist to provide:

  • ethical grounding

  • research-informed perspective

  • psychological safety in design decisions

This page is not intended to present medical endorsement or clinical claims.

Collaboration Overview

Dr. Misuzu Nakashima contributes clinical perspective to the development philosophy behind MyndMap.

The collaboration focuses on:

  • understanding executive dysfunction experiences

  • reducing shame-based interaction patterns

  • supporting sustainable behavioral change

  • aligning design decisions with psychological research principles

Clinical insight informs how MyndMap communicates, rather than diagnosing or treating users.

Role Within MyndMap

The collaboration supports:

  • review of emotional tone in UX language

  • guidance on supportive framing

  • avoidance of harmful productivity pressure

  • refinement of user interaction philosophy

Dr. Nakashima’s involvement helps ensure that MyndMap remains:

  • respectful

  • non-judgmental

  • psychologically considerate

Research-Informed Design

MyndMap applies research awareness through design principles such as:

  • reducing cognitive overload

  • encouraging gradual progress

  • supporting autonomy rather than compliance

  • minimizing negative self-evaluation triggers

These principles influence:

  • task wording

  • reminder behavior

  • AI response tone

  • onboarding experience

The goal is not clinical intervention, but healthier interaction patterns.

Ethical UX Philosophy

A core design rule informed by clinical discussion:

Tools should never make users feel worse for struggling.

Implementation examples:

  • No streak punishment systems

  • No failure language

  • No urgency-based pressure

  • Non-dismissive reminders

This creates an environment where users can re-engage without guilt.

Communication Guidelines (External Use)

When referencing collaboration publicly:

Acceptable Language

  • “Clinically informed”

  • “Developed with clinical insight”

  • “Research-informed design approach”

Avoid

  • medical claims

  • treatment language

  • endorsement wording

MyndMap is a productivity and organization tool — not a medical product.

Cultural Context (Japan)

Authority signals function differently within Japanese markets.

Users often evaluate trust through:

  • association with expertise

  • perceived seriousness of development

  • ethical responsibility

Subtle clinical collaboration communicates:

  • care

  • legitimacy

  • long-term commitment

without requiring explicit promotion.

Intended Effect

When communicated correctly:

  • hesitation decreases for first-time users

  • perceived safety increases

  • paid conversion improves

  • long-term trust strengthens
