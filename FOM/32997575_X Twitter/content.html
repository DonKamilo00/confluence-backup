Purpose

X is used for:

  • Real-time thinking

  • Conversation

  • Light product updates

  • Insight fragments

It is not the canonical source of truth.

Content Types

  • Short insights

  • Threads (derived from blogs)

  • Build-in-public moments

  • Product milestones

Cadence

  • Irregular but intentional

  • No obligation to daily posting

  • Quality > frequency

Silence is acceptable.

Voice Boundaries

  • Founder voice is allowed

  • Brand voice remains calm

  • No performative controversy

Engagement Rules

  • Respond thoughtfully

  • Ignore bait

  • Never argue publicly

  • Do not chase impressions

Golden Rule

If posting increases anxiety or distraction,
don’t post.
