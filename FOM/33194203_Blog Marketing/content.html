Purpose

Blogs are MyndMap’s durable memory.

They:

  • Outlast social feeds

  • Capture nuance

  • Build authority over time

Blog Goals

  • Explain complex ideas clearly

  • Educate without condescension

  • Build long-term trust

  • Support SEO naturally

Topic Selection Framework

A blog topic must answer at least one:

  • What problem does this clarify?

  • What question does this resolve?

  • What feature does this contextualize?

Avoid writing just to “publish”.

SEO (Without Spam)

  • Human-first writing

  • Clear headings

  • Honest titles

  • Internal linking

SEO supports clarity — it does not drive content.

Conversion Strategy

Blogs should:

  • Reference real features

  • Invite exploration, not pressure

  • Act as a bridge to the app

Golden Rule

A good blog should still be useful two years later.
