Fountain of Marketing documents how MyndMap communicates value to the world in a calm, intentional, and sustainable way. It defines how content is planned, created, published, and iterated across blogs, X (Twitter), YouTube, and the website—ensuring marketing reflects the product’s clarity, integrity, and long-term vision rather than short-term noise.

Marketing at MyndMap is treated as translation, not persuasion: turning product truth into language people can understand and trust.
