Purpose

YouTube is MyndMap’s explanatory medium.

It is used for:

  • Depth

  • Visual clarity

  • Long-form understanding

Video Formats

  • Feature explainers

  • System walkthroughs

  • Founder reflections

  • Devlogs (when useful)

Production Standards

  • Clear audio

  • Calm pacing

  • Minimal editing

  • No forced personality

Clarity > polish.

Cadence

  • Low frequency

  • High intention

  • Evergreen value

Funnel Logic

YouTube → Website → App
Not YouTube → hype.

Golden Rule

A video should feel like a calm explanation —
not a pitch.
