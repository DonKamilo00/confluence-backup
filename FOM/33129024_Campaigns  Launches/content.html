Purpose

Campaigns are deliberate events, not constant noise.

When to Run Campaigns

  • Major feature launches

  • Platform milestones

  • Strategic announcements

Not:

  • Minor tweaks

  • Small bug fixes

Launch Framework

  1. Preparation

  2. Soft awareness

  3. Release

  4. Observation

  5. Reflection

Soft vs Hard Launch

  • Soft launch preferred

  • Hard launches are rare

  • Staged exposure reduces risk

Golden Rule

If a campaign adds stress internally,
it’s not worth it.
