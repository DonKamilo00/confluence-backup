About Disrupting Japan

Format: Long-form interview podcast, typically 30–50 minutes

Episodes: 255+ episodes, running since ~2014

Status: First and largest startup podcast in Japan

Audience: Entrepreneurs, investors, VCs, and founders operating in or targeting Japan

Distribution: Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Podbean, Player.fm + newsletter

The show's tagline is "Straight talk from Japan's most innovative founders and VCs." Every episode centres on one founder or investor and goes deep on their story, their market, and what they've learned. Tim asks difficult questions and challenges guests when answers seem vague or promotional.

Listeners frequently describe it as the best resource for understanding the Japanese startup mindset. Former guests report receiving high-quality follow-up contacts — investors, hires, partnerships — directly from the episode audience. The community is engaged and serious.

What he does NOT want:
