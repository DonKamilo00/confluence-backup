🧭Phase 5 — Core Outreach Principles

The goal is conversation — not promotion. Every DM should feel like it came from a person who genuinely read their work. If it reads like a pitch, it won't land.

1.Conversation first

Lead with connection. Never open with features or a product ask. The product comes second.

2.Reference something specific

Before sending, read at least one of their posts. Name it. Generic messages are obvious and ignored.

3.Keep DMs under 100 words

Respect people's attention. Shorter messages are more likely to be read in full.

4.Lead with resonance, not features

"I related to what you wrote" beats "our app has 47 features." Always.

5.One ask per message

One call to action only. Don't ask for a review AND a share AND a reply in the same DM.

6.Warm up before you DM

Engage with their content 3–5 times before sending. You're not a stranger — you're a reader.

7.7-day follow-up rule

If no response, one follow-up after 7 days. Then move on. No repeat chasing.

8.Make your profile credible first

Before sending any DMs, ensure your http://note.com / X profile clearly explains who you are and what MyndMap is.
