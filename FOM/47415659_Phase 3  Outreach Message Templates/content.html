Tier 1 — Writers & Bloggers

Template 1A — Standard Writer Outreach~60 words · soft CTA

Hi — I've been reading your posts about ADHD on note and found them really thoughtful. I built a small system called MyndMap — designed specifically for ADHD reflection and task clarity. It's still early, but I'd genuinely value your thoughts if you'd ever like early access. No pressure at all — just wanted to reach out to people whose writing resonates.

Use for: any Tier 1 contact where you haven't read a specific post yet. Safe default.

Template 1B — Personalised (recommended)Requires reading 1 post first

Hi — I came across your piece on [TOPIC] and it really resonated. I'm building MyndMap — a small ADHD productivity app focused on reflection and task clarity. We're in early access and I'd love to offer you a spot if you'd be open to it. Your writing style feels like exactly the kind of voice that would give useful feedback.

Personalise [TOPIC] with the exact post title or subject. Higher response rate than 1A. Takes 5 mins per outreach.

Tier 2 — ADHD Personal Accounts

Template 2A — Community Peer~55 words · peer tone

Hi — I came across your ADHD posts and related to a lot of what you wrote. I'm building a small ADHD support app called MyndMap and am sharing early access with a few people in the community. It's built around reflection and task clarity — nothing overwhelming. Would you be open to trying it?

Use for: personal experience accounts. Tone is peer-to-peer. Avoid any language that sounds like a product pitch.

Template 2B — Parent / Carer AccountsFor bios mentioning parenting ADHD child

Hi — I've been following your posts about navigating ADHD and they really struck a chord. I'm building MyndMap — a gentle ADHD productivity tool focused on reducing overwhelm. We're giving early access to a small group and I thought you might find it useful. No strings — happy to share more if you're curious.

Adapt when bio mentions parenting/caring role. "Gentle" and "reducing overwhelm" resonate with carer audiences.

Tier 3 — Community Amplifiers

Hi — I've been reading your work and really respect how you've built a genuine ADHD community. I've been quietly building MyndMap — a small app for ADHD reflection and task clarity. We're at early access stage and I'd love your honest take, or just to know if it feels relevant to your audience. No ask beyond a conversation — genuinely curious what you think.
