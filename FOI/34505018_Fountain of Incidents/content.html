Fountain of Incidents is MyndMap’s record of unexpected issues, failures, and near-misses encountered during development and release. Each incident documents what happened, why it happened, how it was resolved, and what was learned — so the same problem doesn’t need to be solved twice. This space exists to improve system reliability, protect users, and strengthen engineering judgment over time through clear, blameless analysis.
